Nordnet AB publishes its third quarter 2026 interim report

Nordnet today publishes its interim report for the third quarter 2026. The full
report is available at www.nordnetab.com

"We delivered our second-best quarter ever, with an operating profit of SEK
1,096 million, an increase of 24 percent from the same quarter last year. We
welcomed 64,000 new customers and are now approaching 2.6 million active savers
on our platform, while our total savings capital reached a new record level of
close to SEK 1.5 trillion", says Rasmus Järborg, CEO of Nordnet.

"During the quarter, we have taken important steps to broaden our offering as we
move towards our goal of becoming European savers' first choice. We have entered
into a partnership with ABG Sundal Collier to create a next generation Private
Banking offering, and launched an outstanding equity research portal together
with Citi. We are preparing the upcoming rollout of a series of ETFs from
Nordnet's own fund company and will shortly be launching our service in the
German market", says Rasmus Järborg, CEO of Nordnet.

Highlights during the quarter

* Continued strong inflow of new savers to the platform, with annual customer
  growth of 12 percent.
* Net savings increased by 23 percent to SEK 25.6 billion for the quarter.
* Strong growth across all revenue streams.
* Good cost control, with the quarter's costs in line with financial targets.
* Operating profit increased by 24 percent to SEK 1.1 billion.
* Announced initiative to launch Nordnet-branded exchange-traded funds (ETFs) in
  early 2027.
* Nordnet and ABG Sundal Collier enter into a partnership to form a jointly
  owned company for the next generation of Private Banking.
* A Research Portal launched in collaboration with Citi, giving Private Banking
  customers access to Citi's research coverage of around 1,500 companies.
* Nordnet celebrates its 30th anniversary as a digital platform for savings and
  investments.

Financial results in brief, July-September 2026 (July-September 2025)

* Operating income SEK 1,550 (1,290) million
* Operating expenses SEK -450 (-403) million
* Operating profit SEK 1,096 (886) million
* Earnings per share after dilution SEK 3.50 (2.81)
