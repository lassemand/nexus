Genmab Announces Financial Results for the Nine Months of 2026

November 5, 2026 Copenhagen, Denmark;
Interim Report for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

Highlights

* Epcoritamab approved in Europe (as TEPKINLY^{®}) and Japan (as EPKINLY^{®}) in
  combination with R² for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or
  refractory FL
* Initiation of two new Phase 3 trials for petosemtamab in first- and
  second-line CRC
* Genmab revenue increased 22% compared to the first nine months of 2025, to
  $3,238 million
  

“The third quarter of 2026 reinforced the breadth of our late-stage portfolio.
TEPKINLY plus R² became the first bispecific-based therapy approved in Europe
for second-line FL, extending epcoritamab's reach as a core therapy across
B-cell malignancies. We also broadened our petosemtamab program with new Phase 3
studies in CRC, underscoring our confidence in this asset beyond head and neck
cancer. As anticipated, we are now in our data-rich fourth quarter, where we
have delivered pivotal readouts across our three late-stage assets, epcoritamab,
Rina-S^{®} (rinatabart sesutecan) and petosemtamab, supporting multiple
regulatory submissions and potential launches in 2027,” said Jan van de Winkel,
Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer of Genmab.

Financial Performance First Nine Months of 2026

* Revenue was $3,238 million for the first nine months of 2026 compared to
  $2,662 million for the first nine months of 2025. The increase of $576
  million, or 22%, was primarily driven by higher DARZALEX^{®} and Kesimpta^{®}
  royalties and higher EPKINLY^{®} net product sales.
* Royalty revenue was $2,699 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared
  to $2,219 million in the first nine months of 2025, an increase of $480
  million, or 22%. The increase in royalties was driven by higher net sales of
  DARZALEX and Kesimpta.
* Net sales of DARZALEX by J&J were $12,410 million in the first nine months of
  2026 compared to $10,448 million in the first nine months of 2025, an increase
  of $1,962 million or 19%.
* Global net sales of EPKINLY/TEPKINLY^{®} were $491 million in the first nine
  months of 2026 compared to $333 million in the first nine months of 2025, an
  increase of $158 million or 47%.
* Cost of product sales were $234 million for the first nine months of 2026
  compared to $157 million for the first nine months of 2025. The increase of
  $77 million, or 49%, was primarily driven by the profit-sharing amounts
  payable to AbbVie related to EPKINLY sales.
* Acquisition and integration related charges related to the integration of
  Merus were $87 million in the first nine months of 2026.
* Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $35 million for the first nine
  months of 2026 compared to $9 million for the first nine months of 2025. The
  increase of $26 million, was primarily driven by the amortization of the Merus
  technology platform.
* Operating expenses were $2,018 million in the first nine months of 2026
  compared to $1,498 million in the first nine months of 2025, an increase of
  $520 million or 35%.
* Adjusted operating expenses, which exclude Acquisition and integration related
  charges and Amortization of acquired intangible assets, were $1,896 million
  for the first nine months of 2026 compared to $1,489 million for the first
  nine months of 2025. The increase of $407 million, or 27%, was primarily
  driven by investment in our product pipeline, including the advancement of
  Rina-S and petosemtamab, and our global commercialization capabilities in
  preparation for their anticipated launches.
* Operating profit was $986 million in the first nine months of 2026 compared to
  $1,007 million in the first nine months of 2025. Adjusted operating profit,
  which excludes Acquisition and integration related charges and Amortization of
  acquired intangible assets, was $1,108 million in the first nine months of
  2026 compared to $1,016 million in the first nine months of 2025.

Key Company Events Post-Quarter End

* October: Positive topline results from the Phase 3 EPCORE^{®} DLBCL-2 trial
  evaluating fixed treatment duration epcoritamab in combination with standard
  of care rituximab, cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin hydrochloride, vincristine,
  and prednisone (R-CHOP), compared to R-CHOP alone, in newly diagnosed patients
  with diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL) with an International Prognostic
  Index (IPI) score of 2 to 5. Data will be submitted for presentation at a
  future medical meeting. Genmab and AbbVie Inc. (AbbVie) will engage global
  regulatory authorities to determine next steps.
* October: Positive topline results from the global Phase 3 RAINFOL™-02 trial of
  Rina-S in patients with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer (PROC) regardless of
  Folate Receptor Alpha (FRα) expression level or prior treatment with
  mirvetuximab. The trial met its primary endpoint of progression-free survival
  (PFS), with Rina-S demonstrating a statistically significant and clinically
  meaningful improvement compared with investigator’s choice chemotherapy in the
  intent-to-treat (ITT) and in the primary analysis set (PAS) populations. Based
  on these results, Genmab plans to engage with regulatory authorities worldwide
  regarding potential next steps. The data will also be submitted for
  presentation at a future medical meeting.
* October: Positive topline results from the global Phase 3 LiGeR-HN1 trial
  (NCT06525220) evaluating petosemtamab in combination with pembrolizumab versus
  pembrolizumab in patients with first-line (1L) PD-L1 positive
  recurrent/metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC). The trial
  met both of its primary endpoints of overall survival (OS) and objective
  response rate (ORR), regardless of HPV status. Based on these positive topline
  results, Genmab plans to engage global regulatory authorities and will submit
  the full results from LiGeR-HN1 for presentation at a future medical meeting.
