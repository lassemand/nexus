Getinge Interim Report July-September 2026: Increased profitability, strong cash flow and higher customer satisfaction

"We delivered organic growth both in order intake and in sales during the
quarter, at the same time we increased profitability and generated strong cash
flow." says Mattias Perjos, President & CEO of Getinge.

Sales growth was primarily driven by Acute Care Therapies while Surgical
Workflows contributed with a stable order intake, mainly within operating room
equipment.

The situation for Life Science remains mixed - the high-margin Sterile Transfer
category performed strongly, while the project-based and capital-intensive
product category of WIS, which includes washer-disinfectors, was impacted by the
persistently low level of investment activity in the pharma segment," says
Mattias Perjos, President & CEO of Getinge.

The adjusted EBITA margin strengthened sharply, rising to 15.1%, and was
positively impacted by tariff refunds of SEK 45 M and year-on-year currency
effects of approximately SEK 36 M. Even excluding these factors, the margin
improved by more than 1 percentage point to 14.3%, which reflects the sustained
positive underlying performance of the operations.

"The improvement was driven by a favorable mix, price discipline and
productivity improvements. Improved profitability, together with a positive
development in working capital, contributed to strong cash flow," says Perjos.

In the latter part of the quarter Getinge initiated the launch of Cardiohelp II
- the medtech company's next generation ECLS therapy platform - in CE countries
and Hong Kong. Customer feedback has been positive.

"We also received 510(k) clearance for a new Edi catheter, which can be used
together with our ventilators to support breathing for premature infants
weighing as little as 500-1000 grams," tells Perjos. "It is increasingly
important for us as a trusted partner in healthcare to show how our solutions
can help increase productivity."

During the quarter, Torin Plan Optimizer launched, an AI-powered system designed
to manage resources more efficiently and make surgical workflows more
predictable during surgeries.

"Getinge's greatest strength lies in the work my colleagues around the world do
every day in close collaboration with our customers. Against the backdrop of an
uncertain global environment, I am therefore particularly pleased that we have
achieved all-time highs in both customer satisfaction and employee engagement.
At the same time, our ongoing dialogue with customers supports our decision to
maintain our outlook for organic net sales growth of 3-5% for the full year
2026," concludes Perjos.

 July-September 2026 in brief

* Net sales increased organically by 2.1% (9.5) and the order intake rose by
  2.6% organically (4.7)
* Adjusted gross profit amounted to SEK 4,343 M (4,051) and the margin was 51.4%
  (49.2)
* Adjusted EBITA was SEK 1,278 M (1,079) and the margin 15.1% (13.1)
* Adjusted earnings per share amounted to SEK 3.10 (2.42)
* Free cash flow amounted to SEK 1,302 M (793)

About Getinge

With a firm belief that every person and community should have access to the
best possible care, Getinge provides hospitals and life science institutions
with products and solutions that aim to improve clinical results and optimize
workflows. The offering includes products and solutions for intensive care,
cardiovascular procedures, operating rooms, sterile reprocessing and life
science. Getinge employs approximately 12,000 people worldwide and the products
are sold in more than 135 countries.
