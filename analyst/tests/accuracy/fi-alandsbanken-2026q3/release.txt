Interim Report for the period January - September 2026


Continued solid development in the core business

“Our business continues to perform well, and we generated a return on equity of
16.9 per cent during the quarter.

“The volume of actively managed assets that we manage on behalf of our customers
contin­ued to record strong net inflows and reached a new all-time high of EUR
12.5 billion. Our three main segments, Private Banking, Premium Banking and IT,
together improved their results compared with the corresponding period last
year.

“We received confirmation of our strong customer relationships in EPSI Rating’s
customer sur­vey of the Finnish banking and finance sector, in which the Bank of
Åland received the sector’s highest ratings for service, quality, customer
loyalty, trust and sustainability.

“We see that our way of serving customers in our segments continues to be
appreciated. During the quarter, we therefore began establishing operations in
Haparanda and Luleå. This initiative is a natural step in our continued growth
in Sweden and builds on the Bank of Åland’s long experience as a full-service
bank in both Finland and Sweden, as well as our understand­ing of the needs that
arise when people and businesses live and operate across national borders.

“Despite the continued uncertainty in the world around us, we see good
opportunities to con­tinue developing our business in the near future."

Peter Wiklöf
Managing Director and Chief Executive

January−September 2026 compared to January−September 2025

* Net operating profit decreased by 11 per cent and amounted to EUR 45.6 M
  (51.0).
* Core income in the form of net interest income, net commission income and IT
  income increased by 2 per cent to EUR 162.0 M (159.1).
* Other income decreased by 69 per cent to EUR 1.5 M (4.8).
* Total expenses increased by 5 per cent to EUR 117.9 M (112.3).
* Net impairment losses on financial assets (including recoveries) amoun­ted to a
  net recovery of EUR 0.0 M (net impairment losses of 0.6), equiva­lent to a loan
  loss level of 0.00 per cent (0.02).
* Return on equity after taxes (ROE) decreased to 15.8 per cent (18.4).
* Earnings per share decreased by 13 per cent to EUR 2.36 (2.71).
* The common equity Tier 1 capital ratio decreased to 12.4 per cent (12.7 on
  December 31, 2025).
* Unchanged future outlook: The Bank of Åland expects its return on equity after
  taxes (ROE) to continue to exceed its long-term financial target of 15 per
  cent during 2026.

The third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter 2025

* Net operating profit decreased by 4 per cent and amounted to EUR 15.8 M
  (16.3).
* Core income in the form of net interest income, net commission income and IT
  income increased by 2 per cent to EUR 52.4 M (51.1).
* Other income increased by 51 per cent to EUR 1.3 M (0.8).
* Total expenses increased by 5 per cent to EUR 37.7 M (35.8).
* Net impairment losses on financial assets (including recoveries) amounted to
  EUR 0.3 M (net recoveries of 0.1), equivalent to a loan loss level of 0.04 per
  cent (-0.00).
* Return on equity after taxes (ROE) decreased to 16.9 per cent (17.1).
* Earnings per share decreased by 4 per cent to EUR 0.82 (0.85).
