Camurus AB: Camurus' Interim Report Third Quarter 2026

"Strong third quarter with important milestones ahead"

Summary third quarter 2026

July - September

* Total revenues increased 28% (25% at CER¹) to MSEK 724 (567)
* Product sales increased 28% (25% at CER¹) to MSEK 582 (455)
* Brixadi^{®} royalty revenue increased 23% (21% at CER¹) to MSEK 137 (111)
* Operating result increased 40% to MSEK 322 (230)
* Cash position at the end of the period BNSEK 4.4 (3.5)
* The US FDA accepted for review the resubmission of the NDA for CAM2029 for the
  treatment of acromegaly. PDUFA target action date set to 18 December, 2026.
* Oczyesa^{®} approved by the TGA in Australia for the treatment of acromegaly

January - September

* Total revenues MSEK 1,959 (1,801)
* Product sales MSEK 1,536 (1,409)
* Brixadi royalty revenue increased 35% (36% at CER¹) to MSEK 370 (274)
* Operating result increased 3% (5% at CER¹) to MSEK 783 (761)
* Financial outlook for 2026 reiterated; revenues and operating result expected
  in the upper half of the ranges of BNSEK 2.6 to 2.9 and BNSEK 0.9 to 1.2¹

Events after the period

* Health Canada issued a Notice of Compliance (i.e. regulatory approval) for
  Oczyesa^{®} XR for the treatment of acromegaly
* Brixadi reached the first sales milestone of MUSD 15, to be recognized in Q4
  2026
* Dosing initiated in CAM2056 Phase 2b study in patients with obesity and
  overweight

1. At constant exchange rate

Fredrik Tiberg, President and CEO:
"Camurus had a record third quarter, with total revenues of SEK 724 million, up
28 percent, and an operating result of SEK 322 million, up 40 percent, with no
milestone revenue in the period. Product sales reached an all-time high SEK 582
million on continued growth for Buvidal^{®}, and Brixadi^{®} royalties increased
23 percent. During the quarter, the FDA accepted the resubmission of the NDA for
CAM2029 for review in acromegaly and Oczyesa^{® }was approved in Australia.
After the period, Oczyesa^{®} XR was approved in Canada, and Brixadi reached its
first sales milestone. Based on performance to date, full-year revenues and
operating result are expected in the upper half of the guided ranges."

About Camurus
Camurus is an international, science-led biopharmaceutical company committed to
developing and commercializing innovative, long-acting medicines for improving
the lives of patients with severe and chronic diseases. New drug products with
best-in-class potential are conceived based on the company's proprietary
FluidCrystal^{®} technology and its extensive R&D expertise. The R&D pipeline
includes products for the treatment of dependence, pain, cancer, and endocrine
diseases. Camurus has operations across Europe, the US, and Australia, with
headquarters in Lund, Sweden. The company's shares are listed on Nasdaq
Stockholm under the ticker CAMX. For more information, visit www.camurus.com and
LinkedIn.
