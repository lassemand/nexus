NKT A/S Interim Report Q1-Q3 2026: Continued solid financial and operational execution

Company Announcement


19 November 2026

Announcement No. 20


NKT CEO Claes Westerlind says:


- In Q3 2026, we delivered solid financial results as we continued to execute on
our order backlog and moved forward with our investment programme. As part of
this, we now expect the additional Transmission capacity in Karlskrona to
commence commercial operation ramp-up in Q2 2027, slightly ahead of the original
plan. Alongside these efforts, we continued to advance our Charging Forward
strategy. At our Investor Day in September, we reaffirmed our strategic
direction and medium-term financial ambitions, presented our capital allocation
priorities and showcased what will be the world's largest high-voltage offshore
cable factory.

Financial outlook for 2026

The financial outlook for 2026 is maintained, but NKT now expects to conclude
the year in the upper end of the operational EBITDA-range. Revenue (standard
metal prices) is expected to be approximately EUR 2.65-2.75bn and operational
EBITDA is expected to be approximately EUR 400-430m.


The financial outlook is based on several assumptions, including:

* Satisfactory execution of high-voltage investments and projects to deliver on
  expected profitability margins
* Satisfactory operational execution across business lines
* Stable market conditions across business lines
* Stable supply chain with limited disruptions and access to the required
  labour, materials, and services.


Lower revenue* due to high comparison base, and operational EBITDA of EUR 111m

Revenue in Q3 2026 amounted to EUR 966m compared to EUR 936m in Q3 2025. Revenue
measured at standard metal prices declined to EUR 673m in Q3 2026 from EUR 726m
in Q3 2025, corresponding to organic growth of -7%. The negative development was
expected and

driven by the Transmission and Grid Solutions & Accessories business lines,
mainly as a result of a high comparison base from execution of the Champlain
Hudson Power Express project and repair jobs in Q3 2025, as well as timing of
projects in execution.


Distribution reported positive organic growth, as the robust development in the
power distribution segment was maintained, and the business line benefitted from
the continued ramp-up of additional medium-voltage capacity in Asnaes, Denmark.


Operational EBITDA declined to EUR 111m in Q3 2026 compared to EUR 119m in Q3
2025. Transmission and Grid Solutions & Accessories reported reduced operational
EBITDA, while it increased in Distribution. The operational EBITDA-margin, based
on revenue at standard metal prices, was 16.6% in Q3 2026, representing an
increase of 0.2 %-points compared to Q3 2025.


At the end of Q3 2026, the Transmission order backlog was EUR 12.4bn (EUR 11.0bn
at standard metal prices), compared to a Transmission order backlog of EUR
13.0bn (EUR 11.6bn at standard metal prices) at the end of Q2 2026. The
reduction in the backlog was driven by project execution during Q3 2026. The
backlog does not include five projects awarded under a framework agreement with
the Dutch-German transmission system operator, TenneT. These awards have an
estimated value exceeding EUR 2.5bn.


Free cash flow amounted to EUR -127m in Q3 2026, mainly driven by a negative
contribution from changes in working capital and the ongoing investments to
expand capacity. At the end of Q3 2026, NKT maintained a robust balance sheet,
with net interest-bearing debt of EUR -471m.


The disciplined and stringent execution of the investment projects to expand
capacity was maintained in the quarter. The additional medium-voltage capacity
in Denmark continued to ramp up, while the other production expansion projects
progressed according to plan. This includes the additional medium-voltage
capacity in Portugal, which gradually will become operational during Q4 2026. As
announced at the Investor Day, commercial operation of the Karlskrona expansion
in Sweden is expected to commence in Q2 2027, slightly ahead of the original
plan, derisking the project and allowing for a more controlled ramp-up. The
additional capacity at the high-voltage factory in Cologne, Germany, and the
second cable-laying vessel are expected to become operational in 2027.
